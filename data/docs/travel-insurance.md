# Travel insurance (Premium)

Premium includes worldwide travel insurance for you while your Premium plan is active.

Cover includes:
- emergency medical expenses up to £10 million;
- trip cancellation up to £5,000;
- lost or delayed baggage up to £1,500.

Each trip can last up to 31 days. Winter sports are covered for up to 17 days per year. Cover applies to customers aged under 70.

The insurance doesn't cover your family members. To make a claim, contact our insurance partner through the app: Premium → Travel insurance → Make a claim.
