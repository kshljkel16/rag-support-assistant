# Sending money within the UK

Transfers to UK bank accounts use Faster Payments and are free on all plans. They normally arrive within seconds, but can take up to 2 hours if the payment is checked for fraud.

Limits:
- up to £25,000 per payment;
- up to £50,000 per day in total.

The first time you pay someone new, we check that the name you entered matches the name on their account (Confirmation of Payee). If it doesn't match, stop and check the details: once a transfer is sent, it usually can't be reversed.
