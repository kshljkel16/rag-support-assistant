# Exchanging money in the app

You can hold and exchange 25 currencies in the app. Exchanges in the app use the same rules as card payments abroad: interbank rate on weekdays within your plan's fee-free allowance, a 0.5% fair-usage fee above it, and a 1% weekend markup (not charged on Premium).

The minimum exchange is £1 or the equivalent in another currency.

Exchanges are instant and can't be cancelled once confirmed. If you exchange back, the new exchange counts towards your allowance again.
