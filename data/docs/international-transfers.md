# Sending money abroad

You can send money to bank accounts in more than 40 countries from the app.

Fees:
- **Standard:** £3 per international transfer, plus the currency exchange rules for your plan.
- **Plus:** 2 free international transfers per month, then £3 each.
- **Premium:** 5 free international transfers per month, then £3 each.

Transfers to EU countries in euros (SEPA) usually arrive within 1 working day. SWIFT transfers to other countries take 1 to 5 working days. The receiving bank or intermediary banks may deduct their own fees from SWIFT transfers.

The maximum for a single international transfer is £20,000.
