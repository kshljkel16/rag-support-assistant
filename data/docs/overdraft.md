# Can I have an overdraft?

Orbit doesn't offer overdrafts, loans or credit cards. Your account works only with money you've added.

Some card payments, such as hotel deposits or fuel, can occasionally take your balance slightly negative, because the final amount is higher than the amount first approved. If this happens, add money to bring your balance back to zero. Orbit doesn't charge interest or fees on a negative balance, but if it stays negative for more than 30 days, we may restrict your card.
