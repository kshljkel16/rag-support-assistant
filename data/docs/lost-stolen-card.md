# My card is lost or stolen

Freeze your card straight away in the app: Cards → select the card → Freeze. Freezing is instant and you can unfreeze the card if you find it.

If the card is definitely lost or stolen, choose "Report lost or stolen". This permanently cancels the card and lets you order a replacement.

Replacement cards:
- the first replacement in any 12 months is free on all plans;
- after that, a replacement costs £5 on Standard and Plus, and is free on Premium.

Delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in the UK and 7 to 10 working days to other countries. You can use a virtual card for online payments while you wait.

If you see payments you don't recognise, report them as described in "Disputing a card payment".
