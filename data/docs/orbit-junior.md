# Orbit Junior (ages 7 to 17)

Orbit Junior is an account for children aged 7 to 17, managed by a parent or guardian who has an Orbit account. The parent opens it in their own app: Profile → Family → Add a child.

Junior includes a free card in the child's name, spending notifications for the parent, and savings goals. Parents can set spending limits and block categories such as gambling.

Junior card limits: £100 per day for card payments and £50 per day at ATMs. Junior accounts can't send international transfers.

When the child turns 18, the Junior account becomes a Standard account in their own name after they complete identity verification.
