# Virtual cards

Virtual cards work like your physical card for online payments and Apple Pay or Google Pay, but have their own card number.

You can have up to 1 virtual card on Standard, 3 on Plus and 5 on Premium. Creating a virtual card is free and instant.

Premium customers can also create disposable virtual cards: the card number changes automatically after every payment, which protects you on websites you don't trust. Disposable cards can't be used for subscriptions.

Virtual cards can't be used at ATMs.
