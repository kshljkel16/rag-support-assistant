# Disputing a card payment

If you don't recognise a payment, or a merchant hasn't delivered what you paid for, first try contacting the merchant. If that doesn't work, you can raise a dispute in the app: open the transaction → "Report an issue".

You must raise a dispute within 120 days of the transaction date.

For payments you didn't make (possible fraud), we freeze your card and may give you a temporary credit while we investigate. Most disputes are resolved within 45 days, but some card-network cases can take up to 90 days. If the dispute is decided in the merchant's favour, the temporary credit is taken back.
