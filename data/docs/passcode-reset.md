# Resetting your passcode or changing phone

To reset your passcode, tap "Forgot passcode" on the login screen. You'll confirm your identity with a selfie and a code sent to your registered email address.

If you get a new phone, install the app and log in with your phone number. If you also have a new phone number, you must update it first: on the old phone, go to Profile → Security → Change phone number. If you no longer have the old phone, contact support, who will verify your identity with a new selfie and your ID document. This can take up to 2 working days.

For security, card payments over £1,000 are blocked for 24 hours after a passcode reset or a phone number change.
