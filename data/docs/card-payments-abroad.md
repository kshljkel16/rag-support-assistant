# Paying by card abroad

When you pay in a foreign currency, Orbit converts it at the interbank exchange rate.

On weekdays there is no markup up to your plan's fee-free exchange allowance (£1,000 a month on Standard, £3,000 on Plus, unlimited on Premium). Above the allowance, a 0.5% fair-usage fee applies.

At weekends (from Friday 22:00 to Sunday 22:00 UK time) markets are closed, so we add a markup of 1% on all plans to protect against rate changes. Premium customers pay no weekend markup.

If a card terminal or ATM abroad offers to charge you in pounds, choose the local currency instead. The merchant's conversion rate is usually worse than ours.
