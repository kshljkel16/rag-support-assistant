# Adding money to your account

You can add money in three ways:
- **Bank transfer** to your Orbit sort code and account number. Free, usually arrives within minutes through Faster Payments.
- **Debit card top-up** in the app. Free up to £3,000 per month; above that, a 0.5% fee applies.
- **Apple Pay or Google Pay** top-up. Same rules as a debit card top-up.

Orbit doesn't accept top-ups by credit card or in cash.

The first card top-up on a new account is limited to £500 while we confirm the card belongs to you.
