# ATM withdrawals and fees

Each month you can withdraw cash fee-free up to your plan's allowance: £200 on Standard, £400 on Plus and £800 on Premium. The allowance resets on the 1st of every month.

Above the allowance, Orbit charges 2% of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum fee of £1 per withdrawal.

Some ATM operators charge their own fee. Orbit doesn't control or refund these fees; the ATM should show them before you confirm.

The maximum you can withdraw is £500 per day on every plan.
