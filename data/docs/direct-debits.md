# Direct Debits

You can set up a Direct Debit with a UK company using your Orbit sort code and account number. It appears in the app once the company sets it up, usually within 3 working days.

You can cancel a Direct Debit in the app at any time. Cancelling stops future collections but doesn't cancel your contract with the company.

If a Direct Debit is collected when you don't have enough money, it's returned unpaid. Orbit doesn't charge for returned Direct Debits, but the company may charge you.

Direct Debits are protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee: if an amount is taken in error, you're entitled to an immediate refund from Orbit.
