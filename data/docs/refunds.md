# Where is my refund?

When a merchant refunds a card payment, it usually reaches your Orbit account within 3 to 10 working days. The timing depends on the merchant, not on Orbit.

A refund appears as a new transaction. If the original payment was in a foreign currency, the refund is converted at the rate on the day it arrives, so the amount in pounds can differ slightly from what you paid.

If 10 working days have passed and the merchant has confirmed the refund, contact us in the app and include the merchant's refund confirmation.
