# Card and account limits

Daily limits on every plan:
- card payments: £10,000;
- contactless payments: £100 per payment (Apple Pay and Google Pay have no per-payment contactless limit);
- ATM withdrawals: £500.

There is no maximum balance on your main account.

You can lower your own limits, or turn off contactless, online or ATM payments, in the app under Cards → Security. You can't raise limits above the amounts shown here.
