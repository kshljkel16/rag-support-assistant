# Opening an Orbit account

You can open a personal Orbit account in the app if you are 18 or older and live in the UK. Orbit Junior accounts for ages 7 to 17 are opened by a parent instead (see "Orbit Junior").

You need:
- a valid passport, UK driving licence or UK biometric residence permit;
- a selfie taken in the app;
- a UK mobile number.

Most accounts are verified within 10 minutes. If we need a manual review, verification can take up to 24 hours. You can't add money or order a card until verification is complete.

Opening an account is free. You start on the Standard plan and can upgrade at any time.
