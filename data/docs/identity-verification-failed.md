# Why did my identity verification fail?

Verification usually fails for one of these reasons:
- the photo of your document is blurry, cropped or has glare;
- the document has expired;
- the name on the document doesn't match the name you entered;
- your selfie doesn't clearly show your face.

You can retry verification up to 3 times in the app. After 3 failed attempts, the account is locked for review and our support team will contact you by email within 2 working days. Please don't open a second account while the first is under review, as duplicate accounts are closed automatically.
