# Closing your account

You can close your account in the app: Profile → Account → Close account.

Before closing:
- move your money out. You can't close an account with a balance, including money in Savings Vaults;
- cancel any paid plan. If you're on an annual plan, the usual refund rules apply;
- download your statements, because you won't be able to access the app afterwards.

Closing is free and usually takes 1 working day. Any pending card payments must settle first. Direct Debits and scheduled payments are cancelled automatically.
