# Savings Vaults

A Savings Vault keeps money separate from your main balance and earns interest. The rate depends on your plan: 3.0% AER on Standard, 3.5% AER on Plus and 4.0% AER on Premium. Rates are variable.

Interest is calculated daily and paid into the vault on the 1st of each month.

You can create up to 10 vaults and move money in or out instantly at any time, with no fees or notice period. The maximum you can hold across all vaults is £100,000.

Money in Savings Vaults is held with our partner bank and protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) up to £85,000.
