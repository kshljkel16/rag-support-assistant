# Scheduled and recurring payments

You can schedule a UK transfer for a future date, or set it to repeat weekly or monthly. Scheduled payments are sent at 06:00 UK time on the chosen day.

If you don't have enough money at 06:00, we try again at 14:00. If the second attempt also fails, the payment is skipped for that date and you get a notification. Future payments in the series stay active.

You can edit or cancel a scheduled payment in the app until 23:59 the day before it is due.
