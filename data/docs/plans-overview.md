# Orbit plans: Standard, Plus and Premium

| | Standard | Plus | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly fee | £0 | £3.99 | £7.99 |
| Free ATM withdrawals per month | £200 | £400 | £800 |
| Fee-free currency exchange per month | £1,000 | £3,000 | Unlimited |
| Virtual cards | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| Savings Vault interest (AER) | 3.0% | 3.5% | 4.0% |
| Travel insurance | No | No | Yes |

Plus and Premium are billed monthly from your Orbit balance. If your balance is too low on the billing date, we retry for 7 days and then move you to Standard.

Annual billing is available for Plus (£39.99 a year) and Premium (£79.99 a year).
