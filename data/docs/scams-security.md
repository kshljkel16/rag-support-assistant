# Protecting yourself from scams

Orbit will never:
- ask for your PIN, passcode or one-time codes;
- ask you to move money to a "safe account";
- ask you to install remote-access software.

If someone contacts you claiming to be from Orbit, the police or another bank and asks you to do any of these, it's a scam. End the call and contact us through the in-app chat.

If you think you've sent money to a scammer, report it immediately in the app: open the payment → "Report a scam". The sooner you report it, the better the chance of recovering the money. Reported scams are reviewed within 15 working days.
