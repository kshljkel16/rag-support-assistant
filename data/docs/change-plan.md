# Upgrading, downgrading or cancelling a plan

You can upgrade at any time. The new plan starts immediately and you pay the difference for the rest of the billing month.

You can downgrade at any time. Monthly plans change at the end of your current billing month; until then you keep your current benefits.

Annual plans: if you cancel within 14 days of starting or renewing, you get a full refund. After 14 days, you keep the plan until the end of the year and aren't refunded.

If you downgrade, vault interest switches to your new plan's rate from the change date, and virtual cards above your new plan's limit are cancelled.
