# Making a complaint

You can complain in the in-app chat, by email to complaints@orbit.example or by post. Please tell us what went wrong and what you'd like us to do.

We acknowledge complaints within 2 working days and aim to give a final response within 15 working days. For complicated cases, we'll explain the delay and respond within 35 working days at most.

If you're unhappy with our final response, or haven't had one after 35 working days, you can refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge within 6 months of our final response.
